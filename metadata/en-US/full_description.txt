ComfyChair H3 is a native Android client for ComfyUI, built around a dedicated MiniMax H3 video workspace. You run your own ComfyUI server; the app puts the whole production loop on the phone.

H3 video workspace

Four phone-oriented H3 templates ship with the app, two of them single-pass and two of them two-pass with an upscale and a second refinement. Modes follow the material you place: a first frame gives image-to-video, adding a last frame gives first-and-last-frame video, text alone gives text-to-video, and references give reference-to-video. Slots hold a first frame, a last frame, up to nine references and up to three audio tracks.

Relay takes queue several segments and start each one as the previous finishes. The auto-written last-frame declaration tells the model that the first image continues the previous segment, checks for an existing declaration first, and can be withdrawn with one tap. A second-pass panel adds an upscale run with its own target size, steps and model. Parameter presets, pre-flight checks and resumable jobs are included.

Image and video

Text to image and image to image, with a style-transfer mode and an inpainting mode that has adjustable brush size and edge feather. Text to video, image to video and video to video, with separate high-noise and low-noise LoRA chains. Up to five LoRAs per chain at any strength from -100 to 100. Length, frame rate and frame count stay aligned to the frame grid the workflow declares.

Prompt tools

A tag library of 2625 words across 39 categories, split into separate SFW and NSFW libraries. Reroll a category, lock a slot, skip a slot for one round, or tap a word to complete it. A four-act story composer with four cast sizes arranges people, setting, action and dialogue into a scene. Prompts can optionally be rewritten by an external model you configure yourself.

Progress you can watch

A status lamp in the corner reports the connection and the model inventory at a glance. A progress bar names the stage and the elapsed time, and says when it is reconnecting instead of leaving you guessing. A sampling preview resolves out of noise while the clip renders, plays the intermediate frames as an animation, and can be cancelled on the spot.

Privacy and storage

Any result can be hidden from the screen with one tap without deleting it. Generated media stays in memory by default and is cleared on exit. The gallery cache keeps thumbnails and full images separately, never evicts favourites, and can clear everything except favourites. After a ComfyUI restart wipes its history, entries with a local copy remain usable, because picking one re-uploads it as a server input.

Server and workflows

Store several ComfyUI servers, confirm a self-signed certificate by its SHA-256 fingerprint once, and sign in with basic auth, a bearer token or browser single sign-on. Follow the queue, cancel or pin your jobs, see what other clients are running, and undo a deletion for a few seconds. A full workflow editor covers the node graph, node values, subgraphs, field mapping and custom fields, and imports and exports ComfyUI UI format, API format and ComfyChair's own format.
